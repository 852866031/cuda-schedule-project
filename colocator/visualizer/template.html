Who knows what (measurement provenance)
INTERCEPTORlibcolocator.so (LD_PRELOAD, client's thread): t_intercept, op kind + args, which client submitted it. Cannot know when anything runs. SCHEDULERlibsched.so issue log (scheduler thread): t_issue, chosen stream, FCFS round order. Launching is async — it cannot know when the GPU starts the op. OBSERVERlibobserver.so (CUPTI activity, passive): t_gpu_start / t_gpu_end, real stream + duration. Joined to the issue log by launch order + correlation id, clocks calibrated.
Not shown: cudaMalloc / cudaStreamSynchronize records (no GPU activity to draw); the sub-µs scheduler round-trip is invisible at most speeds — pick 0.1 ms/s and step near a burst to see queue → stream → running transitions.
